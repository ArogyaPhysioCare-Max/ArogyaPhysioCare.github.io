Our Services

Comprehensive Physiotherapy Treatments

From pain management to advanced rehabilitation – expert care for every need, in-clinic & at home.

Back Pain Treatment

Trusted back pain physiotherapy for muscle strain, poor posture, slip disc and spondylosis. Manual therapy, core-strengthening & posture retraining.

Shoulder Pain Treatment

Physiotherapy for frozen shoulder, rotator cuff tears, impingement and general stiffness. Rebuild range of motion and lasting shoulder health.

Knee Pain Treatment

Knee physiotherapy for osteoarthritis, patellofemoral pain and ligament injuries. Strengthening exercises and gait correction for pain-free movement.

Hip Pain Treatment

Hip pain and bursitis physiotherapy near Adani Shantigram. Manual therapy with targeted strengthening relieves pain and restores hip function.

Foot & Ankle Pain

Physiotherapy for ankle sprains, plantar fasciitis and heel spurs. Strengthening, balance training and taping for pain-free movement.

Arthritis Treatment

Physiotherapy-led relief for osteoarthritis and rheumatoid arthritis. Gentle joint-friendly exercises reduce stiffness and improve daily movement.

Shockwave Therapy

Advanced shockwave for tennis elbow, golfer's elbow, plantar fasciitis and Achilles pain. Portable units available for home visits.

IFT & TENS Therapy

Interferential current and drug-free TENS therapy for chronic pain, nerve-related pain and muscle spasms with clinic-grade portable equipment.

Ultrasound & Combo Therapy

Therapeutic ultrasound combined with IFT or TENS electrotherapy for fast relief from acute muscle spasm and inflammation.

Laser Therapy (LLLT)

Low-level laser therapy to speed up tissue healing and calm pain, swelling and inflammation in injured or post-surgical areas.

Short Wave Diathermy (SWD)

Deep-heat therapy to ease chronic pain, muscle spasms and joint stiffness while boosting circulation in deeper tissues.

Intermittent Pneumatic Compression

Advanced IPC therapy with multi-chamber pneumatic sleeves for lymphatic drainage, reducing edema, and accelerating recovery.

Matrix Rhythm Therapy (MaRhyThe)

Cellular-level vibration to release deep muscle tightness, boost micro-circulation and speed tissue repair. Available at home.

Cervical & Lumbar Traction

Mechanical traction for neck and lower back pain, herniated or bulging discs, and nerve root compression. Safe home delivery.

Post-Surgical Rehabilitation

Structured rehab after knee/hip replacement, fracture surgery, ligament repair. Step-by-step plan rebuilds strength and balance.

ACL Reconstruction Recovery

Post-operative rehab after ACL surgery with progressive strengthening, balance training and functional drills for safe knee stability.

Stroke & Paralysis Rehab

Bed-to-walk rehabilitation for stroke and paralysis patients — from bed mobility to independent walking with gait & balance training.

Neurological Physiotherapy

Specialized physiotherapy for Parkinson's disease, multiple sclerosis and other movement disorders. Focus on balance and independence.

Sports Injury Rehabilitation

Evidence-based sports rehab for ligament tears, sprains, and fast-tracking safe return to sport for athletes and fitness enthusiasts.

Vestibular & Balance Rehab

Vestibular physiotherapy for vertigo, dizziness, BPPV and unsteady gait to improve stability and lower fall risk.

Cardiac & Pulmonary Rehab

Monitored cardiac rehab and chest physiotherapy with airway clearance and breathing exercises to rebuild lung capacity post-COVID.

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Care for infants, children and adolescents addressing developmental delays, motor skill difficulties, posture and injury recovery.

Women's Health / Antenatal & Postnatal

Antenatal & postnatal care, pelvic floor rehab, pregnancy-related back pain, diastasis recti recovery and core strengthening.

Spinal Mobilization & Chiropractic

Hands-on spinal mobilisation and chiropractic-style joint alignment for fast pain relief, disc decompression and improved mobility.

Myofascial Release (MFR) & IASTM

Instrument-assisted soft tissue mobilisation using ergonomic tools to break down scar tissue, ease deep muscle tightness and postural stress.

Maitland & Mulligan Manual Therapy

Evidence-based joint mobilisation techniques to restore normal movement patterns and achieve pain-free function.

Cupping Therapy

Dry and myofascial cupping for back pain, neck stiffness, sciatica and muscle tension to boost circulation and speed tissue recovery.

Kinesio & Lymphatic Taping

Elastic taping to support muscles, stabilise joints, correct posture, and manage post-surgery or post-injury swelling.

Varicose Veins Management

Targeted circulatory exercises, advanced compression therapy and expert guidance to improve blood flow and relieve leg discomfort.

Ergonomic Assessment & Posture

Assessments and posture-correction physiotherapy for neck pain, lower back pain, and workplace strain with custom exercise plans.
